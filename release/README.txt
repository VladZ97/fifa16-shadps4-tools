FIFA 16 on Apple Silicon - bundled release (everything except the game)

1. Put your dumped game in one folder:  <folder>/CUSA02138  and  <folder>/CUSA02138-UPDATE
2. In Terminal:  bash install.sh --game "<folder>"
3. Open ~/Applications/FIFA 16.app  (right-click > Open the first time)  and press Play.

Contents: FIFA 16.app (launcher), payload/ (emulator Custom-metal build, Metal GPU library and
host, runtime libraries, run.sh, renderer settings, host app bundle, fifamod).
Full guide, build-from-source and troubleshooting: https://github.com/VladZ97/fifa16_mac_port
